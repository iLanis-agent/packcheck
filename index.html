PackCheck

You pack the same gym bag, work-trip suitcase, and weekend duffel over and over - and every time you rebuild the list from memory and forget the lock, the charger, the thing you swore you'd remember.

PackCheck keeps your packing lists permanently. Build each list once, check items off as they go in the bag, and hit reset when you're back. Essentials get flagged so the one thing that ruins the trip can't slip through.

No account, no cloud - everything stays in your browser.
